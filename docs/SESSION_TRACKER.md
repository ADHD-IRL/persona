# Session tracker

Session tracker is the main analyst dashboard and the landing page after login. Select one session, then use the same five-step workflow throughout: Overview, Findings, Follow-up, Evidence, Closeout. The radio controls support keyboard navigation; labeled fields, checkboxes, cards and buttons share one visual style. Existing /mitigations bookmarks still open Session tracker.

Overview shows the next useful task, review counts, chains, open actions and overdue work. Open the synthesis or parent analysis brief, and use the progress notice beneath the session selector to follow a running analysis. The session selector discovers new debates about every eight seconds. Active dashboards refresh about every eight seconds; Refresh session retrieves current records on demand. Parent-analysis summaries may combine multiple option debates and are labeled accordingly.

Findings shows records explicitly linked to the selected session, plus chains linked through its threats. Select records and apply a review status in one operation. Inspect evidence and interventions through links that retain session context. Shared parent-analysis findings appear separately. Existing scenario material without session attribution can be explicitly attached; the app never assumes two debates share findings merely because they use the same scenario. New threats extracted from the session workspace now retain their source session.

Follow-up replaces the old action-only dashboard. Add a session-wide action or link one to a finding or chain; define observable completion criteria. Search and filter Open, Overdue or All. Edit the action, date, status, criteria, and completion evidence using the shared controls. Manage your follow-up with status, due dates, completion criteria and evidence. Marking Verified requires completion criteria and verification evidence. Verification does not automatically lower risk. Unattributed scenario actions are shown separately and can be attached when appropriate.

Evidence contains saved round-one assessments, round-two rebuttals and source records for this session. Open the full debate in a new tab when more detail is needed. Sources and model perspectives require analyst verification.

Closeout records your conclusion, decision or disposition, unresolved questions, revisit conditions, and accepted risks or deferred work. Local drafts are retained per session in this browser; Save session review persists them in the database. Review complete requires a completed debate, a conclusion and a decision. If findings or follow-up remain open, document the outstanding work and explicitly acknowledge it. A completed analyst review does not resolve every risk or close the debate's actions. Changes to linked material or scenario context flag a completed review for rechecking after refresh. Download session brief exports Markdown using the current closeout text, findings, chains, follow-up and synthesis, including unsaved edits.


## Search, comparisons, and decision impact

Explore and prioritize provides three tools throughout the selected session. Search this session searches saved SME responses, live-debate messages, findings, chains, follow-up, sources, synthesis, current scenario context, saved analysis inputs, and the current closeout draft. Enter words and choose a content type; expand a result to read its full saved record or open the relevant workflow step. Parent-analysis material is labeled as shared. Search does not fetch unsaved streaming text or other sessions’ debate records. What changed? compares an earlier completed debate in the same scenario, suggesting the most recent one for the same decision option. Select another baseline if needed. Inspect context, analyst conclusions, model synthesis, SME responses, messages, sources, and added, changed, or absent findings and actions. Records match by ID or unique exact name after case/whitespace normalization. Ambiguous or renamed findings stay separate. Absence does not mean resolution, and these are current saved records, not immutable historical snapshots. Decision impact ranks active unresolved findings by your assessment: could reverse the decision, could change its conditions, not assessed, then limited impact. Record the affected criterion, reasoning, what would change the conclusion, and evidence needed next. Save decision impact persists your assessment; resolution requires supporting evidence. Changed findings or conclusions require reassessment. Reviewed findings remain eligible: review is not resolution. Impact assessments are included in the downloaded session brief and changes invalidate a completed analyst review.

Decision-impact assessments are stored per session in workspace-scoped AppConfig under session_impact_<session ID>. These tools use saved local records; they do not invoke the LLM.
